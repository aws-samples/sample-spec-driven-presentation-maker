Part 2 · Rules · 1/2
Category color identifies services; it never decorates the page
Use full category color for identity
Place it on a group border, header band, legend swatch, or small category label. The reader learns the mapping once and scans every architecture faster.
Do not decorate with category color
A decorative purple rule or pink headline falsely signals a service category, so it makes diagrams slower to decode.
Use tint for group fills
Use the matching --cat-*-tint behind services. Put normal --color-text on every tint; reserve the full color for the border and small header.
Keep other colors to the minimum
Beyond ground and text, add only colors that distinguish content. More color invents categories the message does not contain.
Part 2 · Rules · 2/2
One claim, one orange emphasis, and one readable route through the slide
Message
Use a full-sentence finding, sentence case, at most two lines. Keep one claim: a claimless architecture is a map without a legend.
Emphasis
Use Smile Orange once: one number, node, or current step. Because orange can signal warning, two spots read as two alerts.
Icons
Services use official icons from search_assets, source aws. Generic concepts use general icons. Align each icon and short label on a grid; use ground or tint, never a card.
Readability
Text contrast is at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 at 18pt and above. Keep margins at least 5% of the slide edge. Never use emoji.
Charts
Label values directly. Remove gridlines that carry no information. Use a table when exact lookup matters and a chart when shape or change matters.
Renderer facts
Native tables inherit the template font. Slide JSON has no letter-spacing, line-height, or weight 650; use bold and explicit box heights.

Part 3 · Message & Outline · 2/2
Finding-led titles and literal visual forms keep technical choices unambiguous
Good: complete findings

3. One event path decouples every consumer
Bad: labels without claims
1. Target architecture
2. Cost overview
3. Service comparison
Favor
Numbers: one metric or a directly labeled KPI row
Comparison: aligned rows or a hairline table
Process: numbered phases with named services
Relationship: grouped architecture with orthogonal connectors
Use for text
A short claim plus three proof points. Use prose only for a necessary qualification or source note.
Avoid
Decorative circles, gradients, shadows, glow, 3D, diagonal connectors, unlabeled charts, and cards around service icons.

Part 6 · Frame · content
The title zone always reserves two lines, even when this finding uses one
Content begins at y = 300px
Title box: 173px high at y = 64px, enough for two lines of 36pt type. One-line titles leave the second line empty; content never moves up. Horizontal margins are 112px and content width is 1696px.
Bottom rhythm
End primary content by y = 930px. A source line is content and may be drawn at y = 970px. Keep the remaining footer band clear for the template.
