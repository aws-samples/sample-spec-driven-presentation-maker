Rules / read first
One container, one fact, one grid
This deck is scanned before it is read, often on a shared screen by people who joined late. Each fact gets its own rounded container on a quiet grey ground, so any tile can be entered first and still make sense. Order is a convenience, never a prerequisite.
Every slide, every style
  • One claim per slide; the title is that claim.
  • Margins stay at least 5% from every edge.
  • Contrast is ≥ 4.5:1; ≥ 3:1 only at 18pt+.
  • Charts label values directly and drop empty gridlines.
  • No emoji; use a sourced icon only when words fail.
Fixed numbers
  • Use 3–6 tiles on each content slide.
  • Keep every horizontal and vertical gap at 24px.
  • Keep every tile radius at 20px.
  • Use exactly one dark tile; use zero or one accent tile.
  • Keep each tile's fact to 12 words or fewer.
  • Rotation is 0°; never tilt or overlap tiles.
Rules / derived practice
Restraint keeps independent facts readable
Do
  • Vary tile size within the 3–6 tile limit.The largest tile carries the claim's evidence.
  • Snap to 12 columns and 220px rows.Column 118px, gap 24px; n columns span 142n − 24px.
  • Use a label, value, and one qualifier.Keep the fact itself to 12 words or fewer.
Do not
  • Do not nest or overlap tiles.A split fact gets a second tile on the grid.
  • Do not vary gap, radius, or shadow.Size, the dark tile, and the accent tile provide emphasis.
  • Do not draw arrows or connectors.Read left to right, top to bottom; numerals carry sequence.
Test every tile
Can it be read alone, in one breath, and remain true? If not, merge it with its neighbour or give it a slide.

Title grammar
Write an assertion of 8 words or fewer, on 2 lines at most. Compress without losing the claim.
Never use a topic-label title.
Deck shape
Let the brief and material set deck length. One claim per slide means the claim count sets the slide count.
Opening, order, and chapters follow the material; no purpose-specific sequence is imposed.
Density
Use 20–60 visible words over 3–6 tiles. Each tile holds one ≤12-word fact plus one qualifier.
Split rather than shrink.
Title placement and visual choice
Place titles above the grid or in its first dark tile; keep one placement throughout a deck. Favour numbers and parallel categories.
