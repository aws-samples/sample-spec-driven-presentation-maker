Rules / read first
This page is set to be read,
not watched
The deck is circulated and read alone. Print composition outranks stage decoration.
Always
  • One claim per page; the headline is that claim.
  • Keep 120px side margins and 66px foot clearance.
  • Contrast ≥ 4.5:1; ≥ 3:1 only at 18pt+.
  • Label chart values directly; remove empty gridlines.
  • No emoji; sourced icons stay outside prose columns.
Fixed numbers
  • Masthead rule 3px; every other rule 1px.
  • Use 2 or 3 columns, never 4; gap stays 48px.
  • Kicker ≤ 4 uppercase words; headline ≤ 2 lines.
  • One drop cap, only in the first column.
  • Red marks 1 element; rotation is exactly 0°.
Do
  • Open with masthead rule, kicker, hairline and headline.
  • Set justified copy at a fixed measure.
  • Separate zones with 1px edge-to-edge rules.
  • Spend red once: figure, chart mark, rule or cap.
  • Close with a 1px rule and sans folio.
Do not
  • No fill behind prose: no cards or boxes.
  • No third rule weight and no grey rule.
  • No fourth column or variable gap.
  • Never rotate, skew, overlap or tilt a rule.
  • Never set body below 14pt or above 16pt.

Deck shape
Let the brief and material set page count. Opening, sequence and close follow the material; this style imposes no chapter template.
Density
Use 60–120 visible words on a reading page, excluding kicker, folio and table cells. A figure page may fall to 25 words.
Favoured forms
Prefer justified columns, numbered paragraphs, hairline tables, and one large figure or native chart with a caption. A reading page uses 2–3 columns and one first-column cap; a figure page puts one number or chart above its italic caption. The kicker identifies which.

Reading / three columns
Example spread
The first constraint decides
the remaining choices
A short deck line explains how the argument below supports the headline.
P
laceholder prose opens beside the cap and introduces the evidence.
The first column resumes at full measure. It explains the constraint, names the scope, and establishes the terms used in the columns that follow.
The second column develops the argument at matched density. Its prose stays justified and unboxed, allowing the reader to follow the reasoning without visual detours.
The final column closes the reasoning and names the consequence. It does not introduce a second claim; that would require another page.
