Rules / read first
Part two
This page is set to be read,
not watched
The deck is circulated, printed and read alone. Composition therefore follows print.
Always
  • One claim per page; the headline is that claim.A page that argues two things needs two pages.
  • Margins 120px at the sides, 66px at the foot.Both exceed 5% of the slide edge.
  • Contrast 4.5:1 and above; 3:1 only at 18pt or larger.Ink on paper is 16.4:1; red on paper is 5.8:1.
  • Label chart values directly; drop empty gridlines.A reader compares marks, not a legend.
  • No emoji; sourced icons never inside a column.Prose is the illustration on this page.
Fixed numbers
  • Masthead rule 3px; every other rule 1px.Two weights are the whole structural vocabulary.
  • Columns 2 or 3, never 4. Column gap 48px, fixed.Measures: 816px at two columns, 528px at three.
  • Kicker 4 words, uppercase; headline 2 lines.The kicker names the page; the headline argues it.
  • Drop cap on the first column only, once a page.A second cap turns an opening into a pattern.
  • Red on 1 element a page; rotation exactly 0 degrees.Labels of a red mark stay ink. Body type 14–16pt.
Every limit on this page is a number, so it can be checked
Rules / derived practice
Part two
Structure comes from rules
and measure, not panels
Do
  • Open every page with rule, kicker, hairline, headline.The repeated masthead makes a sequence of pages one paper.
  • Set body copy justified at the fixed measure.Dense prose is correct here: 60–120 words on a reading page.
  • Separate each zone with a 1px rule, edge to edge.The rule replaces the border of the card you are not drawing.
  • Spend the red once: a figure, a bar, a rule, a cap.Scarcity is what makes the single red read as emphasis.
  • Close with a 1px rule and a folio line in sans.The folio is the only place small sans type belongs.
Do not
  • Do not fill a shape behind text: no cards, no boxes.Fills are for data marks and one table header band only.
  • Do not invent a third rule weight or a grey rule.3px and 1px in ink; a lighter rule reads as a printing fault.
  • Do not use 4 columns or a variable column gap.An uneven measure destroys the only grid this style has.
  • Do not rotate, skew, overlap, or tilt a rule.Permitted rotation is exactly 0 degrees.
  • Do not set body copy below 14pt or above 16pt.Cut words instead; on a read page the measure wins.
Test of every choice: would a compositor set it that way?

Message / deck shape
Part three
A reading page carries more
words than a spoken one
Length
Build 10–18 pages. Opening, sequence and close follow the material; this style imposes no chapter template of its own.
Density
Set 60–120 visible words on a reading page, excluding kicker, folio and table cells. A figure page may fall to 25 words.
Favoured forms
Prefer justified columns, numbered paragraphs, hairline tables, and one large figure with a caption.
A page is either a reading page or a figure page, and its kicker says which
On a reading page prose is the evidence: 2 or 3 justified columns, one drop cap on the first.
On a figure page one number or one chart holds the upper zone, with an italic caption under it.
Density is a rule of the composition, not a licence to overfill
Palette / paper and ink
Part four
Paper, ink, and one red
spent once a page
Aged paper is the ground. Ink sets every word and rule. Red marks one thing.
Paper #F5F1E8
--color-bg. The ground of every page, and the only surface used.
Ink #151515
--color-text and --color-border. Type, folios, both rule weights.
Red #B3261E
--accent. One element a page: a figure, a bar, a rule, a cap.
Aged ink #5C574C
--color-muted. Captions, folios, the weaker side of a pair.
Tint #ECE6D8
--color-surface. One table header band. Never behind prose.
Ink on paper is 16.4:1, red on paper 5.8:1, aged ink 6.3:1. The swatches above are documentation, not permission to fill a shape.
Red is never a category, a sign, or a second accent. No gradient and no opacity exist anywhere in this style.
Two colours and one tint — counted, not mixed

Frame / standard page
Part six
The masthead repeats so the
pages read as one paper
The five fixed marks
  • Masthead rule: left 120, top 70, width 1680, height 3.The only 3px rule on the page.
  • Kicker at top 88; folio right at left 1400, top 90.Sans, uppercase, one line, 4 words maximum.
  • Hairline at top 126 closes the masthead band.Everything above it is identical on every page.
  • Headline box: top 150, height 156 — two lines at 32pt.A one-line headline leaves line two empty.
  • Hairline at 322 opens the content zone at 350.Foot: hairline at 966, folio at 984.
How the parts are built
Every rule is a line element, or a rectangle 1px or 3px tall in ink. Every column is its own text box: slide JSON has no multi-column text.
A drop cap is the same story: there is no drop-cap setting, so it is three boxes. Pattern seven gives their coordinates.
The content zone runs 350 to 946 px, 596 px tall — about twelve lines of 15pt body copy. A column needing more space is a page needing splitting. Leave the foot band clear so the template may set its own page number.
