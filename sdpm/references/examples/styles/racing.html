PART 2 / RULES
CONTROL THE VELOCITY
Design decision: readers must capture the claim at a glance, so motion comes from a slanted headline and a few horizontal lines—not from rotating, rounding, or multiplying decoration.
DO / HOLD THE LINE
  • Put exactly one 10px tricolour stripe at the top of every slide.
  • Keep one claim per slide; the title is that claim and uses no more than 5 words.
  • Keep all margins at 5% of the slide edge or greater.
  • Maintain text contrast of at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 for text at 18pt and above.
  • Label chart values directly and remove gridlines that carry no information.
DON'T / BREAK TRACTION
  • Use red on no more than 1 content element per slide, excluding the fixed stripe.
  • Use no more than 3 speed lines per slide; every speed line is exactly 2px high.
  • Use italics on headlines only; body text remains upright.
  • Use 0° rotation, no yellow, no rounded corners, no shadows, and no extra decoration.
  • Use no emoji and no colour beyond the minimum needed to distinguish content.
NEXT LAP — WRITE THE CLAIM SEQUENCE
PART 3 / MESSAGE & OUTLINE
BUILD FORWARD MOMENTUM
Use short, verb-led claims so every title feels like acceleration. There is no fixed chapter shape; the look governs cadence, not subject matter.
01 / LAUNCH
Lead with a verb
Use 5 words or fewer and no topic-only labels.
02 / HOLD
Show one claim
Use one dominant visual form and 25–60 words.
03 / EXIT
Name the next step
End every slide with the action or question that advances the sequence.
04 / FINISH
Close at speed
Resolve the final claim without adding a recap grid.
DECK LENGTH
Use 8–16 slides. Split any slide that needs a sixth title word or a second claim.
VISUAL RHYTHM
Alternate open metric, divided comparison, and linear evidence forms; do not repeat one composition three times.

Beyond the fixed top stripe, red marks at most one word, value, bar, rule, node, or state. White carries headlines; grey carries upright evidence.

PART 6 / FRAME
BUILD THE RACE LINE
FIXED FRAME
Stripe: exactly one group at x=0, y=0, width=1920px, height=10px. Build it as three adjacent rectangles: blue x=0–640px, violet x=640–1280px, red x=1280–1920px. Reserve y=70–280px for a title of at most 2 lines. Content begins at y=300px or below; side margins are 120px.
Section labels stay upright. The title alone uses italic bold. The footer names the next lap without a filled band.
SPEED-LINE RECTANGLES
Use 0–3 rectangles, each exactly 2px high. Give each a linear gradient from transparent white at stop 0 with opacity 0 to white at stop 1 with opacity 1. Align them horizontally; shorten each successive line from the left. Do not rotate them.
