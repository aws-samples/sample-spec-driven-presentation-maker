Control the velocity
Readers must capture the claim at a glance. Motion comes from a slanted headline and a few horizontal lines—not rotating, rounding, or multiplying decoration.
Do / hold the line
  • Put exactly one 10px tricolour stripe at the top of every framed slide.
  • Keep one claim per slide; the title is that claim, in 5 words or fewer.
  • Keep margins at least 5% of the slide edge.
  • Maintain contrast ≥ 4.5:1, or ≥ 3:1 at 18pt+.
  • Label chart values directly; remove gridlines without information.
Do not / break traction
  • Use red on more than one content element, excluding the stripe.
  • Use more than three speed lines; each is exactly 2px high.
  • Italicise evidence; italics belong to headlines only.
  • Rotate, use yellow, round corners, add shadows, or decorate.
  • Use emoji or colour beyond the minimum needed to distinguish content.
Build forward momentum
Use short, verb-led assertion titles so each feels like acceleration. Never use topic labels. The look governs cadence, not subject matter.

Chapter shape
Let brief and material set deck length. Split a slide needing a sixth title word or second claim; there is no fixed chapter shape.
Visual rhythm
Alternate open metric, divided comparison, process, native chart, and spare table forms; do not repeat one composition three times.
