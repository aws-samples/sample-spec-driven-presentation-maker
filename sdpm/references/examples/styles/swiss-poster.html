Composition over decoration
Rules / form and counts
ONE SHAPE
PER SLIDE
Projected large and read from the back, each slide is a poster: one oversized red shape anchors an exposed asymmetric grid. Composition, not inflated type, carries the image.
  • Exactly one red shape: circle 280–720px; square 280–560px; bar 96–240px thick; or diagonal band 96–200px thick by 1200–1400px long.
  • Alternate silhouettes; never repeat one on consecutive slides.
  • Use 1–2 black grid lines: a 1px full-span hairline or the 8px left bar. Never three.
  • At most one vertical label at 11pt and two body blocks, each at most five 16pt lines.
  • Slide titles are 1–2 lines at 36–44pt; cover titles are at most three lines at 68pt. Type never becomes the bleeding image.
  • Only the shape may cross one or two edges. Type crosses none and keeps at least 96px / 5% clear.
  • A diagonal band runs top edge to bottom edge at −18° or +18°; keep all type outside its 300–600px swept corridor.
  • Derive the grid: column n begins at 96 + 144(n−1)px; n modules span 144n − 24px.
  • Margins stay at least 96px. Contrast is ≥4.5:1, or ≥3:1 at 18pt+.
02 / NN
Rules / do and do not
COUNT IT BEFORE
YOU BUILD IT
Do
  • Make one claim per slide and make the title that claim.
  • Keep the white ground and visible black grid.
  • Label chart values directly on marks; retain only the informative baseline.
  • Use white words on red only when the one shape is a bar or square.
Do not
  • Add a fourth colour, tint, or second accent: white ground, black type, red shape, muted grey secondary labels only.
  • Set red type; add gradients, shadows, rounded corners, or emoji.
  • Rotate anything except the vertical label and diagonal band.
  • Add chart legends or gridlines when labels and one baseline carry the evidence.
03 / NN
Message and outline
SHORT PHRASE,
SINGLE CLAIM
Title and density
  • Use a 2–6 word noun phrase, or a numbered title whose numeral joins the composition.
  • One claim per slide; supporting copy uses one or two blocks and never restates it.
  • At most six discrete items. A seventh item or a third body block means split the slide.
  • Keep title boxes reserved for two lines even when only one is used.
Sequence and visual choice
  • Let audience, time, and material set deck length.
  • Cover → continuously numbered claims, with numbered section dividers when needed → closing implication. Never restart numbering.
  • Vary circle, square, bar, and band to create rhythm without wallpaper.
  • Favour one large figure, two- or three-part comparison, short numbered process, single native chart, or spare table.
  • Section dividers pause with no body; the close states one implication, owner, date, or invitation.
