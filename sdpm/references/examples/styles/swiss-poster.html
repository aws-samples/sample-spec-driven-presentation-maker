Rules / read first
ONE SHAPE
PER SLIDE
Design decision: this deck is projected large and read from the back of a room, so every slide is built as a poster. One oversized red shape anchors the composition and the grid is left visible instead of hidden. Scale alone does not carry a slide here: remove the shape and the hairlines and nothing is left to look at, which is why titles stay at 36-44pt and never run off an edge to become the image.
Per slide, exactly 1 red shape, from 4: circle 280-720px across; bar 96-240px thick; diagonal band 96-200px thick and 1200-1400px long, fixed at -18deg or +18deg; square 280-560px per side.
Vary the shape from slide to slide so two consecutive slides never repeat one silhouette.

Rules / counts
COUNT IT BEFORE
YOU BUILD IT
Per slide, 1-2 black grid lines: a 1px hairline (--rule-w) running the full width or height, or the 8px left bar (--bar-w). Never three.
Per slide, at most 1 vertical label at 11pt and at most 2 body blocks of 5 lines at 16pt.
Slide title 1-2 lines at 36-44pt; cover title up to 3 lines at 68pt.
The red shape may cross 1 or 2 slide edges and is the only element allowed to. Type crosses none and keeps 96px, 5% of the edge, clear.
The diagonal band always runs from the top edge to the bottom edge, and its rotation sweeps a corridor 300-600px wide that no type enters.
03 / 22
Rules / do and don't
WHAT TO DO,
WHAT NEVER TO DO
DO: make one claim per slide and make the title that claim. Derive coordinates from the grid: column n starts at 96 + 144(n-1)px and a span of n modules is 144n - 24px wide. Keep margins at 96px or more. Keep text contrast at 4.5:1, or 3:1 at 18pt and above. Label chart values on the bars and draw no gridline but the baseline.
DON'T: add a fourth colour, a tint or a second accent. The palette is #FFFFFF ground, #111111 type, #E30613 shape, and #6B6B6B for secondary labels. Never set red as type: the one place red carries words is white text on a red bar or square. 0 gradients, 0 shadows, 0 rounded corners, 0 emoji, and rotation only on the vertical label and the band.

Message / title grammar
TITLES ARE
SHORT NOUN PHRASES
Write the title as a short noun phrase of 2-6 words, or as a numbered title where the number joins the composition.
One claim per slide, and the title is that claim.
Supporting copy sits in one or two blocks and never restates the title.
05 / 22
Message / outline
ONE CLAIM EACH,
ONE POSTER EACH
Let the brief and material set the deck length; vary the four shapes so repetition builds rhythm without becoming wallpaper.
Density per slide: 1 title, at most 2 body blocks of 5 lines, and at most 6 discrete items. A seventh item means the slide splits.
Numbering is a design element, not a footnote: every slide carries NN / NN in the bottom-left module at 10pt, and a section divider sets its number at cover size.
The sequence never restarts inside a deck.
Favour forms the shape can anchor: one large figure, a two- or three-part comparison, a short numbered process, a single chart.

Section label placeholder
STANDARD FRAME
FOR CONTENT SLIDES
The section label sits in the top-left module at 10pt. The title takes two lines below it and never moves down to centre itself.
Content begins at --content-top, 376px, whether the title used one line or two.
The numbering closes the bottom-left module.
The vertical label runs up the right margin at x 1784px. Composers build it as a textbox with textDirection "vert270", which sets the text bottom-to-top; the box is 40px wide, 320-480px tall, and carries 2-5 uppercase words.
