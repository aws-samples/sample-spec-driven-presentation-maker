Rules / read first
The gradient is a pointer: it marks what the title is talking about
In a lit room the white field carries the reading and the gradient does the pointing.
Four roles, and only four
  • A key number — gradient text on it.The figure is the claim, so it is marked.
  • The chosen item — outline and pill.4px gradient outline, filled gradient pill.
  • The highlighted bar — gradient fill.That one bar only; the rest stay flat.
  • No claim — the signature bar alone.Covers, agendas and dividers end here.
No quota; what stays flat
  • Signature — the 40px left-edge bar.Vertical, three stops, on every slide.
  • Metadata — an outline pill, no fill.A date, a team, an index. Never emphasis.
  • Everything else — flat.Solid fills, 2px borders, muted labels.
  • One subject may be marked twice.A number and the bar that is it. Not two.

Rules / derived practice
A gradient that decorates stops being able to point
Do
  • Mark what the title names, nothing else.A second subject makes the slide ambiguous.
  • Keep gradients to text, lines, pills, bars.A mark smaller than its subject stays a mark.
  • Keep the direction fixed.Horizontal for text and pills; vertical bars.
  • Give sibling items a muted label.Siblings stay flat so one mark stays one.
Don't
  • No full-bleed gradient, tile or card fill.A large field is a mood, not an aim.
  • No blur, glow, radial ramp or fade.A soft edge cannot mark a boundary.
  • No hue outside the three stops.A rival colour splits the pointing.
  • No gradient for status or category.It already means "look here".
Test every gradient: say the title out loud; if the sentence does not name what you marked, make it flat. One claim per slide; margins 5% or more; contrast 4.5:1, or 3:1 at 18pt or 15pt bold; values labelled; no emoji.

Frame / building the roles
Each role is one shape property, so nothing has to be faked
Stroke widths are given in slide pt with the demo px beside them.
Four roles, four shapes
Signature bar. A rectangle, no line, gradient fill, angle 90, stops 0 #8B7CFF, 0.5 #6D28D9, 1 #EC4899.
Pointed card. A rounded rectangle, fill none, gradient line at angle 45, line width 2 pt (4 px in this demo), corner adjustment 32 ÷ shorter side.
Filled pill. Gradient fill, angle 0, two stops, corner adjustment 0.5, height 56 px, label 15pt bold #FFFFFF.
Outline pill. The same shape, fill none, gradient line at angle 0, line width 1 pt (2 px in this demo), label #4C1D95.
Marked bar. Gradient fill, angle 90, three stops, width 128 px; the flat bars are solid #E9E6F5.

Text
The saving is real but it is concentrated in the quiet hours
One claim, one figure, three supporting points.
What the measurement found
Across twelve months the service was under a tenth of its peak load for two thirds of the clock. Removing that idle capacity is where the whole reduction comes from.
  • Quiet hours carry the saving; nothing changes at peak.So a workload busy all day will not see this result.
  • The window is a full year, including two seasonal peaks.A single quarter would have overstated the effect.
  • Scope is unchanged: same requests, same boundary.No work was moved out to make the number smaller.
