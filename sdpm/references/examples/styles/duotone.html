PART 2 / RULES
One edge decides it
  • Cut the slide with 1 edge.
  • Use 1 of the 4 splits.

  • 1 title, 6 words, 2–3 lines.
  • Orange words, dark field only.

DO NOT / KEEP THE CUT HARD
Read from the back of a room: contrast does the work a grid, a card or a shadow would do elsewhere.
  • 0 second edges, 0 crossings.
  • 0 cards, panels or badges.
  • 0 white type on orange.
  • 0 grey on the orange field.
  • 0 greys in body copy.
  • 0 shadows, radius, rotation.
PART 2 / LIMITS
Count the parts
Every number opposite is a ceiling, not a target. A slide that needs a fifth split or a third block is two slides.
Contrast is measured, not assumed: white on near-black 16.8:1, near-black on orange 5.2:1, orange on near-black 5.2:1, grey on near-black 7.4:1.
NUMERIC LIMITS
  • 1 split, from 4 allowed

  • 30–70 words per content slide

PART 3 / MESSAGE
The edge is the argument: claim on one field, proof on the other. Decide which field holds the claim before writing a word.
TITLE GRAMMAR / ONE CLAIM
Two fields, two jobs
Six words, one assertion
Write the title as one assertion of 6 words or fewer, sentence case, 2 lines at most. At 40pt a 1088px field holds about 22 Latin or 13 Japanese characters per line; a 448px field holds 9 and 5, so a title there takes 3 short lines instead.
A second claim needs a second slide.
PART 3 / OUTLINE
Length follows the material
DECK SHAPE
1 CLAIM
Per slide. The brief and material set the total.
WORDS PER SLIDE
30–70
Words including the title. Two fields hold two thoughts.
SPLIT RHYTHM
Order the claims by the argument, then assign splits: long evidence takes the 67% field, one measure the 50/50, a wide table the horizontal split. Turn the edge every 3 or 4 slides.

ON THE ORANGE FIELD
Orange #FF4F00

A white shape may stand here: the highlighted bar of a chart is white at 3.3:1, which the 3:1 limit for non-text allows. Type never is.

PART 6 / GRID
One grid per field
Full-height field: label y=88, title y=144 in a 240px zone, content from y=440, nothing below y=984. Inset 96px from both field edges.
Horizontal field, 540px tall: label y=72 or y=590, a one-line title, content 270–440 or 640–920. The field without the title starts its content at the title's y.
REPEATED ON EVERY SLIDE
One 11pt label tops each field — orange on the dark field, near-black on the orange one. No page number, no footer, no logo strip.
A coloured lead word is two textboxes: the lead in orange at 900, the body beside it at 400. Slide JSON has no inline colour change.
Bars are rectangle shapes with direct labels — a native chart colours a whole series, never one bar.
