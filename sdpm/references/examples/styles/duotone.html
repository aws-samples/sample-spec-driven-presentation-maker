PART 2 / RULES
One edge decides the page
  • Cut each slide with exactly 1 edge.
  • Take that edge from the 4 splits.
  • Claim on one field, proof on the other.
  • 1 title per slide, 2 lines at most.
  • 2 body blocks per field, no more.
  • White on near-black, near-black on orange.
  • Size makes hierarchy: 1 largest item.
  • Label every value and unit directly.
DO NOT / KEEP THE CUT HARD
Read at distance, often on a screen nobody can walk up to: contrast does the work a grid, a card or a shadow would do elsewhere. So the slide is one straight cut across two flat fields, and nothing may soften it.
  • 0 colours beyond the two fields.
  • 0 greys, tints or second accents.
  • 0 cards, panels, badges or outlines.
  • 0 elements across the edge, 0 second edges.
  • 0 gradients, shadows, rotation or radius.
  • 0 white text on orange — it reaches 3.3:1.
  • 0 emoji; fill icons with one field colour.
  • 0 gridlines that carry no information.
PART 2 / LIMITS
Count before you build
Every number opposite is a ceiling, not a suggestion. A slide that needs a fifth split, a third colour or a third body block is two slides.
Contrast is measured, not assumed: white on near-black reaches 16.8:1 and near-black on orange 5.1:1, so both pass at every size used here.
NUMERIC LIMITS
  • 1 split per slide, from 4 allowed
  • 2 colours per slide, 0 greys
  • 1 title, 2 lines, 6 words
  • 2 body blocks per field
  • 30–70 words per content slide
  • 0 boxes, 3 hairlines of 2px per field (relationship connectors are the one exception: up to 8, all 2px)
  • 1 image per slide, 6px frame
  • 0° rotation, 0 shadows, 0px radius
  • 96px across and 72px down from every edge
PART 3 / MESSAGE
The edge is the argument: claim here, proof there. Decide which field holds the claim before writing a word, then keep that decision for the whole slide.
TITLE GRAMMAR / ONE CLAIM
Two fields, two jobs
Six words, one assertion
Write the title as one assertion of 6 words or fewer, in sentence case, 2 lines at most. This is arithmetic, not taste: at 44pt a 768px field holds about 16 characters per line and a 1088px field about 22. Drop the articles before you drop the verb.
One claim per slide, and the title is that claim. The opposite field answers only that claim: a measure, a set of three, a table, a chart. A second claim needs a second slide, never a second split.
PART 3 / OUTLINE
Length follows contrast
DECK LENGTH
12–20
Slides. High contrast tires the eye sooner than it tires the mind, so split content across more slides rather than filling a field.
WORDS PER SLIDE
30–70
Words on a content slide, title included. Two fields hold two thoughts; a third thought is the next slide.
SPLIT RHYTHM
No chapter shape belongs to this style. Order the claims by the logic of the argument, then assign splits: long evidence takes the 67% field, a single measure the 50/50, a wide table or a four-part set the horizontal split. Open on the cover split and close on the same one; in between, turn the edge every 3 to 4 slides so the deck keeps a beat without any slide needing two edges. A section change is a title slide, not a divider graphic.
PART 4 / PALETTE
Two colours, no middle
Near-black #1D1D1B
The ground, applied to the whole deck. Text on it is white at 16.8:1. It is also the text colour used on the orange field, so the deck needs no third value anywhere.
There is no surface or muted colour here: both tokens resolve to the ground and to white on purpose, so nobody can reach for a grey. Go smaller instead.
Orange #FF4F00
One field per slide — never a highlight, a bullet, a badge or a rule inside the other field. Text on it is always near-black at 5.1:1; white on orange reaches 3.3:1 and is not used at any size.
Set fontColor to #1D1D1B on every element standing on the orange field: the deck default is white, so an unset element disappears. Photographs keep their own colours — there is no two-tone filter to apply — so place one image inside a single field with a 6px frame in that field's text colour, and never across the edge.

PART 6 / FRAME
One rectangle makes the split
The near-black ground arrives with the deck. Draw exactly one orange rectangle on it, full height or full width, with no line, no radius and no shadow. Take its coordinates from the eight rows opposite. A slide that seems to need another edge is a slide carrying two claims.

    orange 0,0,1920,540 or 0,540,1920,540

PART 6 / GRID
Same grid inside every field
Vertical splits, full-height fields: label at y=88, title at y=144 in a 200px zone, content from y=392, nothing below y=984. Inset 96px from both field edges.
Horizontal split, 540px fields: label at y=72 or y=612, a one-line title at y=120 or y=660, content from y=290 or y=660, nothing below y=468 or y=1008.
REPEATED ON EVERY SLIDE
One 12pt uppercase label sits at the top of each field. No page number, no running footer, no logo strip — each would have to hug or cross the edge and would blunt it. The 96px inset gives a 448px column in a 640px field, 1088px in a 1280px field.
The title always stands on the larger field; on a 50/50 either may take it. The field without the title starts its content at y=144, or y=120 in a horizontal field. A closing slide is the same construction with one word.
