Part 2 · Rules
The slide is a surface the room works on
Participants read on screen or paper while discussing. Each page must stay useful for minutes, not seconds, and leave enough visual air for people to think and speak.
Keep content inside 10% margins.
The section label and source line may sit in the outer band.
Hold density near 70%.
Whitespace leaves room for discussion.
Read phases left to right.
Show shared progress before detail.
Pair icons with short phrases.
Paired rows scan faster than bullets.
Make the title the one claim.
Discuss one proposition at a time.
Accent marks only the current phase.
One signal answers “where are we?”
Show a question or decision zone.
Make the room’s next contribution explicit.
Separate with lines and distance.
Open columns invite annotation.
Part 2 · Guardrails
Clarity comes from restraint, not compression
No dense paragraphs.
Narrative competes with discussion; move it to notes.
No more than two content rows.
A third row breaks the 70% density limit.
No type below 16pt.
Room and print readers need the same page.
No decorative shadows.
Leave shadows off.
No more than one saturated colour.
Multiple signals dilute shared focus.
No emoji.
Use searched icons or simple geometry.
No unnecessary colour.
Add colour only to distinguish content.
No weak contrast or ornamental charts.
Keep contrast, direct labels, and useful gridlines only.
Composer checkCould two more people discuss this page without either leaning forward to read?
Part 2 · Renderer facts
Build the same restraint into slide JSON
Native tables
They inherit the template font; do not set a per-table font-family. Accept that font for this style.
Filled shapes
Leave shadows off; this style uses tint and hairlines, never depth.
Type refinements
Slide JSON has no letter-spacing, line-height, or weight 650. Approximate 650 with bold and preserve rhythm with explicit text-box heights.
Use searched icons rather than emoji. Keep the template footer band from y=988 to 1080 empty.

Part 3 · Writing grammar
Titles may ask the exact question the room must resolve
Use a short assertion by default. A question title is allowed only when it is the exact issue the room must resolve.
Good titles
The handoff is the main delay
Which option can we support now?
Bad titles
Process overview
Some thoughts and considerations
Choose visuals by job
Numbers: 3 across.   Compare: matrix.   Process: 3–5 steps.   Roles: phase grid.   Text: icon rows or table.
Title testCan a participant repeat the proposition without reading the rest of the slide?

Test the trade-offAsk what the group is willing to own.
Record the implicationConvert the discussion into a next move.
